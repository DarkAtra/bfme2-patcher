BFME2 Accelerator
https://github.com/OhadBaehr/bfme2-accelerator
by Ohad Baehr (studied in Clement Julia's fork, https://github.com/ClementJ18/bfme2-accelerator, branch edain-compat)

game-patcher's render thread is a port of its design: recording the game's Direct3D calls for a second thread, the kind and
argument layout of every method (transcribed from its gen_rt_hooks.py), staged buffer and surface locks. The uncapped frame rate
takes its approach of frames that only draw.
